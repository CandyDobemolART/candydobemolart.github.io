Welcome to CandyDobemol\_ART. \

Candy Dobemol is a musician and artist whose artistic work is inspired by the wonderful world of music, especially guitars. \

\

This gallery brings together his original artwork, paintings and creative designs. A space where art, colour and creativity become unique pieces. \

\

On this website You can discover and purchase his paintings on canvas, as well as explore links to the T-shirt shop featuring his designs. \
