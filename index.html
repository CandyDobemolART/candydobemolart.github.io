About

Welcome to CandyDobemol_ART.

Candy Dobemol is a musician and artist whose artistic work is inspired by the wonderful world of music, particularly the guitar.

This gallery brings together his original artworks, paintings and creative designs, where art, colour and creativity come together to create unique pieces.

On this website, you can discover and purchase his paintings on canvas, as well as explore links to the T-shirt shop featuring his designs.

You can also listen to a couple of his tracks by clicking the music button.

Enjoy!
